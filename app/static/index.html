Enable ADB on Fire TV

1
Open Fire TV Settings

From your Fire TV home screen, go to Settings ⚙️ → My Fire TV → About.

2
Unlock Developer Options

Highlight your Fire TV device name and press the center Select button on your remote 7 times until you see "No need, you are already a developer".

3
Enable ADB Debugging

Go back one screen to My Fire TV, open Developer Options, and turn ADB Debugging → ON.

4
Authorize Computer on TV

Connect from this app. An on-screen prompt will appear on your TV. Check "Always allow from this computer" and select OK.
